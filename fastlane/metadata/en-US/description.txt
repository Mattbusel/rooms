Rooms is a home inventory you can actually finish. Stand in a room, photograph what is in it, type a name and what it would cost to replace. One afternoon covers a house, and the record is ready the day something goes wrong.

Insurers and consumer groups have said for years that everyone should have one. Almost nobody does, because it felt like homework. On a phone with a camera it is thirty seconds an item.

ROOM BY ROOM
• Rooms as labelled boxes, with a running total on each
• Photo from the camera or your library, brand, serial number, quantity, purchase date, price, replacement value, condition, where the receipt is
• Quick-add chips for the common things with typical values already filled in
• Search by name, brand or serial number

WOULD THE POLICY PAY?
• Enter your contents cover, deductible and the caps most policies put on jewelry, cash, firearms and art
• A coverage check shows your total against the limit and each capped category against its cap, with a red flag where you are over

VALUE TODAY
• Replacement cost beside estimated cash value, worn down over each category's useful life
• The high-value list shows exactly what is missing for a claim: no serial, no photo, no proof

THE CLAIM REPORT
• A multi-page PDF, room by room, with thumbnails, serials, totals and a signature line
• A spreadsheet export for the adjuster
• An after-a-loss checklist so the first day is not spent guessing
